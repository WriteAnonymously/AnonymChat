Why this exists

The room before
the résumé.

Most social software asks you to perform an identity before you can say anything. AnonymChat reverses that order.

You enter a conversation under a temporary room name. Public rooms show only current live presence. Private rooms use personal, one-time invitations.

Anonymous does not mean invisible: messages are stored in room history, and the server still operates like a normal web service. It means the interface does not turn your profile into the point.
